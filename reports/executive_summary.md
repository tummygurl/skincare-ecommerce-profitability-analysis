# Executive Summary: Skincare E-store Targets, Margins and Discounts, 2020 to 2023

**For:** Head of E-commerce and Finance Director
**Decision:** Where to cut discounts, and which markets and categories to fix to reach next year's targets.
**Data note:** Synthetic or relabelled data (see the README). Two duplicate rows excluded. Margins are on net sales (what the customer paid).

## Bottom line

Sales grew every year and beat the 20% growth target, but **profit fell 12% in 2023** ($318,675 to $279,937) as margin dropped from 21.1% to 15.0%. The clearest and most fixable leak is **discounts above 30%, which lost $295.7K over four years**.

## What happened

- **Growth target met.** Gross sales grew 22.4%, 26.0% and 23.8% in 2021 to 2023. Net sales rose from $978K in 2020 to $1.87M in 2023.
- **Profit did not follow sales in 2023.** Margin fell in the three profitable categories, in every market (including Europe, where profit still grew on much higher sales), and in every discount band, including sales with no discount (33.4% to 28.1%). Discounting alone does not explain the drop, and the data has no cost fields to say what does.
- **The decline is concentrated.** Profit changed by -$22.8K in Asia Pacific and -$17.7K in LATAM, while Europe grew $8.8K. Asia Pacific's margin fell from 19.6% in 2021 to 9.0% in 2023.
- **Two categories never reach the 15% margin target.** Hair care earned about 1% (and -2.6% in 2020). Home and Accessories lost money every year, $57.2K in total. Body care, Face care and Make up stay above 15%, but each lost roughly 9 to 14 points of margin in 2023.
- **Other targets were missed.** Corporate growth was 20.5%, 26.6% and 24.3% against a 30% target. Africa never reached $400K in sales, and LATAM and USCA reach it only on gross sales in 2023.

## Recommendations

1. **Cap discounts at 30%.** Lines discounted above 30% lost $112.2K in 2023 on $224.4K of net sales, roughly 40% of that year's profit. Capping them would raise 2023 profit by an estimated **$112K to $121K** and lift margin from 15.0% to roughly 20% to 24%. The profit gain holds even if every capped sale is lost; net sales fall only if fewer than about 69% of those sales survive the cap.
2. **Investigate Asia Pacific first.** It is the largest market, and its margin fell to 9.0%. Cost and pricing data are needed to find the cause.
3. **Review the six loss-making subcategories** (fragrances, brushes and applicators, body moisturizers, face masks and exfoliators, candles, sprays and diffusers, Accessories), which lost about $85K combined over four years.
4. **Define the targets precisely.** The brief does not say whether sales and margin targets are gross or net. The answer changes whether LATAM and USCA meet $400K, and whether 2023 margin is above 15%.

## Limits

The cap estimate covers 2023 only, assumes retained sales earn a 2.6% margin, and ignores any effect on full-price purchases. All figures come from synthetic data, so treat them as a demonstration of method, not as results for a real company.
